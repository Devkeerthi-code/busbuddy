# ETAs look wrong

**When:** The ETA is far off from reality.

1. Check the route shape follows the road the bus really takes. If not, after one real trip use **Follow the real road** in the route editor.
2. Check stops are in travel order and on the right side of the road; a stop placed 200 m off the road projects to the wrong distance.
3. Check the timetable times; delay is only as good as the schedule.
4. ETAs improve on their own after 3+ completed trips per segment, when history starts to blend in.
