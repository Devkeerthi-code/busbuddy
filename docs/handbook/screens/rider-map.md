# Live map

**File:** `src/app/rider/map.tsx` · **For:** Students and staff

The route on a map with the bus gliding along it and the rider's stop highlighted.

## On the screen

- Driven part of the route dimmed, the part ahead in the accent colour.
- A draggable bottom sheet ("Trip details") listing stops with arrival times and which were passed.

## States

- No stop chosen yet: "Pick your stop first" with a button back.
- Bus not started: the route and stops without a bus marker.
